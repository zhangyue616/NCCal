# Ordered and whole-layer replacement

All physical channels are ranked using development data only. Environment, atom-type, development-mean and same-molecule displacement controls are compared under frozen BACE and ESOL heads in both encoders. Whole-layer variance explained is variance weighted, not the average of per-channel R-squared. Ordered curves retain the development ordering and all random-order controls.

The twelve prespecified output comparisons form one Holm family. Raw predictions and per-molecule deviations support CPU recomputation. Large residual output changes, the ChemBERTa task cases where shuffling is better, and task-head refits are retained rather than selected away. Random-network and rotated comparisons are descriptive where specified. Timing amendments and the resumed computation do not change scientific endpoints.
