# Deviations and scope

Historical molecular calibration is retrospective on exposed model profiles. The eight-checkpoint family and independent MoLFormer layer-selection family have separate thresholds. The local assembly changes filesystem interfaces only; it does not create a new historical freeze. Original hashes remain historical evidence and are not rebound to the distribution source. The standalone checkpoint smoke is limited to the ChemBERTa native measurement pipeline, not all nine architectures.
