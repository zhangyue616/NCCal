# Frozen transfer, reconstruction and replacement

Development and confirmation molecule identities, concept choices, signs and fitted rules are fixed before confirmation scoring. Development contains 5,000 molecules and confirmation 10,000. The 71 supported concepts and twelve-concept prediction panel are fixed on development. Concept transfer is computed from the saved network-by-rotation-by-concept log profiles and their molecule-bootstrap replicates.

For focal neurons 293 and 454, radius-one bonding environments shrink to atom type and then the development mean with prior count 20. Predictions do not use held-out activations. Continuous pooled-atom R-squared centers each reported stratum on its own target mean. Replacement changes verified heavy-atom anchors, leaving other positions fixed. The shuffled control reassigns prediction-minus-observation displacements within each molecule; it does not shuffle raw activations. All molecule-level observations stay together in resampling.

Nine-neuron panels in each encoder are independent selected panels. Joint BACE and ESOL comparisons use frozen task heads and measured output fidelity. The carbonyl rewriting failure, negative-stratum disadvantages, matched-dose intervals spanning zero and the three panel exceptions are retained. Model-output fidelity is distinct from measured molecular properties.
