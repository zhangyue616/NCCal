# Native-channel and cross-architecture protein cases

All coordinate and sign choices are locked on development proteins. E1-150M uses post-SwiGLU input to w2 in single-sequence mode; AMPLIFY and fixed published readouts keep their own native definitions. E1 channels 2192 and 735 are in block 15; 2062 and 1900 are in block 20. Helix-versus-strand and helix-start-versus-interior are separate labels and signs.

Same-chain same-amino-acid pairs compare helix with strand, or positions one to two of an observed helix of length at least ten with interior positions at least three residues from both ends. Groups, natural-sequence identity, observable windows and original development/confirmation/external roles remain fixed. Group-averaged accuracy differs from pooling all pairs; ties receive one half. Local sequence increments use group-separated development fitting, and per-candidate Holm families remain distinct.

The subsequent external frame and five-position localization tests retain frozen channels and coefficients. The thirteen groups supporting both original E1 contrasts are reused later and are not the new 140-pair frame. Within-helix start pairs are ordered along the sequence; their discrimination is not credited beyond known sequence order. Sheet-topology, published-unit transfer, cross-architecture and chronological replication results remain separate, including null or adverse increments.

The original source byte digests, file sizes, preserved freeze timestamps and the corresponding English protocol are listed in frozen_records.json. Date stamps identify freezes; they do not denote separate reproduction outputs.
